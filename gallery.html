Sunshine Rara Lake
Rara Lake was included as part of Rara National Park in 1976. In 1976, citizens of the two villages Chapra and Rara were resettled to Nepalgunj. Currently, this area along with the national park is being protected by the Nepali Army.
Jomacho
Jamacho Gumba is among the most hiked places in Kathmandu. The ideal location and a manageable trail make this place a hiker’s favorite.
Mugu Village
Mugu is known for being both the most remote district in Nepal, as well as the least developed. Pulu is one of the more well-known villages of the Mugu district.
MUGU Jungle
Mugu District, a part of Karnali Province, is one of the seventy-seven districts of Nepal.
KALINCHOK
CABLE CAR
The Kalinchok Cable Car, Nepal's shortest cable car route, transports visitors from Kuri Village to the Kalinchok Bhagwati Temple, a popular religious and tourist destination known for its panoramic views of the Himalayas.
DURANTA
The Duranta flower, from the Duranta genus, is a fast-growing, tropical shrub known for its vibrant purple or blue blooms and golden berries, often used as an ornamental plant in gardens and landscapes for its beauty and ability to attract butterflies.
CHAMELEON
A chameleon is a distinctive lizard known for its ability to change color, its long, sticky tongue for catching prey, and its independently moving eyes, which provide a wide field of vision.
KURI VILLAGE
Kuri Village, located near Kalinchok in Nepal, is a scenic settlement known for its picturesque views of the Himalayas, traditional lodges, and as a base for pilgrims visiting the Kalinchok Bhagwati Temple, especially popular during winter for its snow-covered landscapes.
RARA Lake
Rara Lake, also known as Mahendra Lake, is the largest fresh water lake in the Nepalese Himalayas. It is the main feature of Rara National Park, located in Jumla and Mugu Districts of Karnali Province.

THARU SOCIETY
Tharu women, part of the indigenous Tharu community in Nepal's Terai region, are known for their vibrant traditional attire, intricate jewelry, unique cultural practices, and key roles in agriculture and family life, with a rich heritage of resilience and environmental knowledge.
Horses at Rara Lake
You can hire a trained horse to ride from Talcha airport to Rara Lake, or from Rara Lake to Murma top. You can see horses grazing in the area around Rara Lake.
